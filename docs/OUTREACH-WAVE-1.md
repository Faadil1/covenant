# COVENANT — Wave 1 Discovery Outreach

Do not lead with “AI”, “blockchain middleware”, or the COVENANT architecture.
Lead with one concrete workflow and ask for the current process.

## TreasurySpring — Matthew Longhurst / innovation-product

**Subject:** Question on mandate continuity for mobilised FTFs

Hi Matthew — I’ve been studying TreasurySpring’s Canton work, especially the move from static FTF holdings toward collateral mobility.

I’m researching a narrow workflow: when an existing cash investment becomes unsuitable for a new use because of collateral eligibility, counterparty, term, liquidity or policy constraints, where is the decision encoded today that determines whether another instrument still preserves the original treasury mandate?

I’m not looking to pitch a generic tokenization product. I’m trying to understand the current operational control: who decides, what evidence is required, and which parts can or cannot be safely automated.

Would you be open to a short 20–30 minute conversation? I’d be happy to keep it entirely around your existing workflow and use an anonymized example.

Hi — M0’s emphasis on optionality around reserve storage, yield and contract evolution raises a question I’m researching.

## Follow-up after 5–7 days

Hi — following up once in case this is relevant. I’m specifically looking for one real workflow where an asset/representation became unsuitable and a team had to decide whether to hold, replace, freeze, redeem or escalate.

Even a 15-minute conversation would be useful. If this sits with someone else on product, treasury, operations or risk, a redirect would be greatly appreciated.

## Interview opening

Start with:

> “Before I explain what I’m building, can you walk me through the last time an existing asset or representation became unsuitable because of eligibility, liquidity, custody, rights, redemption or collateral constraints?”

Do not pitch until they have described the workflow.

## Stop conditions

If the response is:
- “our existing OMS/compliance/wallet stack already handles this end-to-end,” ask exactly how;
- “this almost never happens,” quantify frequency;
- “we would never automate replacement,” ask which decisions remain human and why;
- “interesting idea,” do not count as validation.
