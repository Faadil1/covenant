# COVENANT — Design Partner Target Map
**Date:** 2026-09-22  
**Purpose:** execute the Customer Reality Gate without re-opening the Stocklana concept.

## Outreach principle

Do not pitch “COVENANT” first.

Lead with the workflow:

> When an institution wants to preserve an economic mandate while the eligible instrument, wrapper, chain, custodian, liquidity path or collateral status changes, where is that decision encoded today — and what prevents automation from changing the meaning of the position?

Ask for the last real incident before describing the product.

## Wave 1 — highest signal / highest accessibility

### 1. Superstate
**Why now:** USTB is one economic fund position that can exist as book-entry shares or tokenized shares across Ethereum, Solana and Plume. Superstate also exposes protocol mint/redeem and DeFi integrations.  
**COVENANT hypothesis:** preserving one fund mandate across multiple representations/networks while respecting eligibility, redemption availability and protocol-specific constraints is a direct representation-continuity problem.  
**Best roles:** Jim Hiltner (Co-founder, Head of Business Development); Dean Swennumson (Co-founder, Head of Operations); product / FundOS leadership.  
**Ask:** “When the same fund can be held in several representations, how do clients encode which representation is currently acceptable and when software may move between them?”  
**Evidence:** https://superstate.com/assets/ustb ; https://www.superstate.com/about

### 2. OpenEden
**Why now:** TBILL is a regulated tokenized Treasury fund with BNY-managed/custodied underlying assets, whitelisted transfers and a redemption queue; OpenEden is expanding across chains and additional fixed-income products.  
**COVENANT hypothesis:** an institutional cash mandate can remain stable while transferability, whitelist status, redemption timing, chain availability or product eligibility changes.  
**Best roles:** Jeremy Ng (Founder & CEO); product / institutional partnerships / operations.  
**Ask:** “Where do your institutional users decide whether TBILL is still the right cash representation versus another instrument when liquidity, eligibility or operational constraints change?”  
**Evidence:** https://docs.openeden.com/tbill ; https://docs.openeden.com/tbill/redemptions ; https://openeden.com/news/openeden-launches-tbill-hybond-bnb-chain/

### 3. Centrifuge
**Why now:** Centrifuge tokenizes funds, treasuries and credit, supports lifecycle management, qualified custody via Kraken Institutional, and JTRSY is being used as eligible collateral in M0.  
**COVENANT hypothesis:** their ecosystem already has the exact transition from “hold this asset” to “use this representation as collateral / treasury / liquidity” across multiple institutional workflows.  
**Best roles:** Bhaji Illuminati (CEO & Co-founder); Anil Sood (Chief Strategy Officer & Co-founder); product / institutional integrations.  
**Ask:** “As the same institutional asset moves through custody, collateral and DeFi workflows, what determines whether a representation still satisfies the owner’s mandate?”  
**Evidence:** https://centrifuge.io/ ; https://centrifuge.io/blog/kraken-centrifuge ; https://centrifuge.io/blog/m0-centrifuge

### 4. M0
**Why now:** M0 provides modular stablecoin infrastructure where reserve/collateral choices can evolve; it has explicitly positioned optionality around contracts, yield and reserve storage as product value.  
**COVENANT hypothesis:** stablecoin issuers are a natural buyer for a runtime that keeps a reserve mandate true while eligible reserve instruments and collateral representations change.  
**Best roles:** Luca Prosperi (CEO & Co-founder); Joao Reginatto (Chief Strategy Officer); reserve / product / risk leadership.  
**Ask:** “When a stablecoin program can use multiple eligible reserve assets, where is the rule that decides whether a replacement preserves the reserve mandate rather than merely being technically allowed?”  
**Evidence:** https://www.m0.org/ ; https://www.m0.org/press-releases/m0-centrifuge ; https://www.m0.org/press-releases/m0-raises-series-b-with-investment-from-polychain-and-ribbit-capital-bringing-total-funding-to-100m

### 5. TreasurySpring
**Why now:** 1,100+ investment-grade cash products, 120+ counterparties and explicit investment-guideline filtering; it is actively working on tokenized collateral mobility through Canton and says these are concrete client-driven use cases.  
**COVENANT hypothesis:** TreasurySpring may be the cleanest non-crypto validation target because its users already express cash mandates, eligibility schedules and collateral constraints across many interchangeable instruments.  
**Best roles:** Matthew Longhurst (Co-founder & Chief Innovation Officer); product / API / institutional solutions leadership.  
**Ask:** “You already let clients upload investment guidelines. When an existing holding becomes suboptimal or ineligible for a new collateral use, could that mandate safely authorize a representation/instrument transition without a human rebuilding the trade?”  
**Evidence:** https://treasuryspring.com/platform ; https://treasuryspring.com/insights/why-we-are-working-with-canton-on-mobilising-ftfs-treasuryspring

## Wave 2 — strong strategic fit

### 6. Ondo Finance
**Signal:** USDY is a permissionless bearer asset but primary mint/redemption remains jurisdiction-restricted; OUSG is restricted to accredited/qualified purchasers; products are expanding across chains.  
**Hypothesis:** legal eligibility and economic exposure can diverge from simple token transferability — exactly a Claim Passport / lifecycle problem.  
**Best roles:** Ian De Bode (CEO & President); Matthieu de Vergnes (Global Head of Institutional); John Hoffman (Head of Product Portfolios).  
**Evidence:** https://ondo.finance/team ; https://ondo.finance/usdy ; https://ondo.finance/

### 7. Securitize
**Signal:** $4B+ tokenized assets, BUIDL used as institutional collateral and expanded across chains/liquidity integrations.  
**Hypothesis:** as tokenized funds acquire more venues, chains, custody models and collateral uses, economic-state admissibility becomes distinct from minting/transfer-agent infrastructure.  
**Best roles:** tokenization product, digital transfer agency, institutional partnerships.  
**Evidence:** https://investors.securitize.io/news/news-details/2026/Securitize-Reports-Second-Quarter-2026-Results/default.aspx

### 8. Franklin Templeton Digital Assets / Benji
**Signal:** BENJI is a regulated money-market fund using public blockchains as recordkeeping infrastructure, available across multiple networks, with P2P transfers and off-exchange collateral programs.  
**Hypothesis:** one regulated fund exposure already spans network, custody, collateral and trading contexts — a strong continuity/lifecycle use case.  
**Best roles:** Digital Assets & Innovation; Benji platform product; institutional distribution.  
**Evidence:** https://digitalassets.franklintempleton.com/benji/ ; https://www.franklintempleton.com/press-releases/news-room/2026/franklin-templeton-and-binance-advance-strategic-collaboration-with-institutional-off-exchange-collateral-program

### 9. MoonPay Institutional
**Signal:** MoonPay Trade now lets institutions move between supported stablecoins and BENJI exposure through onchain routing/execution.  
**Hypothesis:** routing answers “how”; COVENANT could supply “under what mandate and post-state conditions should this transition happen?”  
**Best roles:** institutional product / trading / partnerships.  
**Evidence:** https://www.moonpay.com/newsroom/moonpay-franklintempleton

### 10. Kraken Institutional
**Signal:** qualified custody now supports tokenized RWA positions that can be held, earn yield, borrow, trade or deploy into vaults from one custody relationship.  
**Hypothesis:** asset utility changes without changing the client’s underlying economic objective, requiring eligibility and authority controls across use cases.  
**Best roles:** custody product, institutional product, RWA partnerships.  
**Evidence:** https://blog.kraken.com/product/kraken-institutional/centrifuge-rwas-in-qualified-custody

## What counts as a qualified signal

**Strong**
- “We already have this problem.”
- concrete incident or manual workflow;
- specific team owns it;
- existing spend or build effort;
- they can name facts required before substitution;
- willingness to share an anonymized case;
- design-partner or pilot discussion.

**Weak**
- “Interesting.”
- “AI agents will need this someday.”
- generic tokenization enthusiasm.
- willingness to take a demo without describing current pain.
